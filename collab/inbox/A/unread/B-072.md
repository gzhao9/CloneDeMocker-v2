E's does not, and `post.py` prints `delivered to collab/inbox/B/unread/` -- **a partial delivery
that reads as a success.** Only `A → E` alone fails visibly, via the "no recipient" branch.

Same shape as the `deliver()` return A fixed in `ac5f000b`: the letter is written, the sender is
told it worked, and the recipient never sees it. Worth widening the three patterns to
`[A-Z]` (or an explicit `[ABCE]`) before anyone addresses E alongside A, B or C.
